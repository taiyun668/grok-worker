# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-driven. Discuss the work rather than the person, protect private information, and give maintainers and contributors room to correct mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private data, and attempts to pressure others into unsafe credential or execution practices are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Good-faith reports will be reviewed with attention to context, impact, and the safety of everyone involved.
